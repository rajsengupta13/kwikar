Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Starwebx Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

1 Overview

Starwebx Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Kwikar", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Kwikar platform — a home appliance repair service connecting customers with verified technicians in all over India.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information. By using the Kwikar platform, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of personal information when you register or use the Kwikar platform:

  • Identity Information: Full name and profile details.
  • Contact Information: Mobile phone number and email address.
  • Location Information: City, area, and pincode to match you with nearby technicians.
  • Account Credentials: Encrypted login PIN used to secure your account.
  • Service Details: Appliance type, issue description, and booking history.
  • Device Information: Browser type, device model, and operating system for platform compatibility.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, and interaction logs to improve our service.

3 How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • Account Creation & Authentication: To register and verify your identity on the platform.
  • Service Delivery: To match your booking request with an appropriate technician in your area.
  • Communication: To send booking confirmations, service updates, and support responses via SMS or notification.
  • Platform Operations: To process payments, maintain service records, and handle disputes.
  • Service Improvement: To analyse usage patterns and improve the platform's features and reliability.
  • Legal Compliance: To meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4 Payment Processing

Kwikar does not directly collect or store payment card details. All payment transactions are processed through secure third-party payment gateways (such as Razorpay) that are PCI-DSS compliant.

Your financial data is handled exclusively by our payment partners and is subject to their respective privacy policies. Kwikar only receives confirmation of successful or failed transactions.

5 Sharing of Information

Kwikar does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Technicians: Your name, contact number, and service address are shared with the assigned technician to facilitate the booking.
  • Service Providers: With payment processors, SMS providers, and cloud hosting services that assist in delivering our platform.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government authority.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred to the acquiring entity.

6 Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encrypted data storage, access controls, and secure communication protocols.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7 Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting our support team. We may retain certain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes (such as resolving disputes).

8 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from promotional communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the details provided on our Contact Us page.

9 Cookies & Local Storage

Kwikar uses browser local storage and session data to maintain your login state, save preferences (such as your pincode), and improve your experience on the platform. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the platform after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
