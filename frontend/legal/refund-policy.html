Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Starwebx Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

1 Overview

This Refund & Cancellation Policy describes the conditions under which refunds may be requested and processed for payments made on the Kwikar platform. Please read this policy carefully before making any payment.

By completing a payment on the Kwikar platform, you acknowledge and agree to the terms described in this policy.

2 Technician Subscription Plans

Technicians on the Kwikar platform may subscribe to paid plans to access booking assignments and platform features.

  • Subscription fees are generally non-refundable once the plan has been activated and platform access has been granted.
  • Activation is considered complete at the time of successful payment confirmation.
  • Partial refunds for unused plan periods are not provided.
  • If a plan is not activated due to a technical error on our end, you may contact support for assistance.

We recommend reviewing all plan details and features before completing your subscription purchase.

3 ABD Membership Fees

Area Business Developers (ABD members) pay a membership fee to join the Kwikar business partner network.

  • ABD membership fees are generally non-refundable once membership access has been granted and activated.
  • Access is considered granted at the time of account activation following successful payment.
  • If membership access was not granted due to a technical failure on our platform, please contact support.

4 Failed & Disputed Transactions

In the event that a payment is deducted from your account but the corresponding plan or membership is not activated:

  • Please contact our support team within 7 days of the transaction date.
  • Provide your registered mobile number, transaction ID, and a brief description of the issue.
  • Our team will investigate the matter and, if confirmed, process the appropriate resolution within the timelines stated below.

Failed transactions are typically auto-resolved by the payment gateway within 5–7 business days. If the amount is not returned within this period, please reach out to us.

5 Refund Processing Timeline

Where a refund is approved by Kwikar, the following timelines apply:

Refund Type Processing Time
Approved refunds (Technician Plan / ABD) 7–10 business days from approval date
Failed transaction — amount not credited 5–7 business days (auto-resolved by payment gateway)
Failed transaction escalated to support 7–10 business days from escalation date

Refunds are credited to the original payment source (bank account, UPI, or card). Kwikar does not issue refunds in cash or via alternative payment methods.

6 Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be provided in the following circumstances:

  • The plan or membership has already been activated and used.
  • Account suspension or termination due to violation of Kwikar's Terms & Conditions.
  • Refund request submitted after 7 days of the transaction date.
  • Change of mind after a plan or membership has been successfully activated.

7 How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund or cancellation request, please contact our support team with the following information:

  • Registered mobile number
  • Transaction ID or payment reference number
  • Date of transaction
  • Reason for the refund request

8 Changes to This Policy

Kwikar reserves the right to modify this Refund & Cancellation Policy at any time. Updates will be reflected by the revised date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
